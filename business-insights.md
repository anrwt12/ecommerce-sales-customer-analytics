# Business Insights

## 1. Overall Business Performance

The business generated approximately ₹5.93B in net revenue from around 250K orders and 39.9K customers, with approximately 312K units sold. The overall scale indicates a large and active customer base, but further analysis is required to understand which categories, customers, locations, and products are driving this revenue.

**Business Action:** Focus on the highest-value revenue drivers while improving customer retention and order value.

---

## 2. Monthly Revenue and Order Performance

March 2026 generated the highest monthly revenue at approximately ₹248.03M, while October 2025 recorded the highest order volume with 10,368 orders. Since the month with the highest orders was different from the month with the highest revenue, order volume alone does not determine business performance.

**Business Action:** Analyze high-revenue months for changes in product mix and average order value, and replicate successful strategies during lower-performing months.

---

## 3. Category Performance

Electronics is the company's dominant revenue category, contributing approximately ₹4.31B or 72.61% of total revenue. Fashion generated the highest order volume with 49,802 orders but contributed only ₹333.68M in revenue, indicating significantly lower-value purchases.

**Business Action:** Protect Electronics as the core revenue driver while increasing Fashion's average order value through bundles, cross-selling, and premium product offerings.

---

## 4. Brand Performance

HP leads brand revenue with approximately ₹942.15M, followed closely by Noise at ₹914.26M. Nike recorded the highest unit sales with 20,155 units but generated only ₹176.81M in revenue, indicating that high unit volume does not necessarily translate into high revenue.

**Business Action:** Prioritize high-revenue brands while evaluating whether high-volume, lower-value brands can be improved through premium products or cross-selling.

---

## 5. Order Status and Returns

Most orders are successfully delivered, but Sports has the highest cancellation rate at 5.14%, while Books has the highest return rate at 5.13%. These category-level issues may indicate problems related to product expectations, delivery, or purchasing decisions.

**Business Action:** Investigate cancellation and return reasons in Sports and Books before increasing inventory or marketing spend in these categories.

---

## 6. Geographic Performance

Uttar Pradesh leads the state-level analysis with 32,631 orders, 5,208 customers, and approximately ₹768.35M in revenue. At the city level, Madurai leads in order volume and revenue, while New Delhi generates similar revenue with fewer orders, suggesting stronger order value.

**Business Action:** Maintain strong investment in high-performing regions while identifying what drives higher-value purchases in cities such as New Delhi.

---

## 7. Customer Revenue Concentration

The highest-revenue customer generated approximately ₹10.71 lakh, while the top 10 customers individually contribute only a small percentage of total revenue. This indicates that revenue is broadly distributed across the customer base rather than being dependent on a small group of customers.

**Business Action:** Continue broad customer acquisition while using targeted loyalty and retention strategies to increase the lifetime value of high-value customers.

---

## 8. Overstock Risk

Several products have very high inventory but relatively low sales. For example, Nescafe Coffee V4 has 1,000 units in stock but only 143 units sold, while Nivea Cream V1 has 997 units in stock but only 79 units sold.

**Business Action:** Avoid unnecessary replenishment of these products and consider promotions, discounts, or bundled offers to reduce excess inventory.

---

## 9. Stockout Risk

Several products have relatively low inventory but higher sales compared with the overall product average. These products may face potential stockout risk if demand continues.

**Business Action:** Prioritize replenishment of high-demand products to avoid lost sales and maintain product availability.

---

## 10. Payment Preference Analysis

This analysis will compare payment preferences across gender and states to identify differences in customer payment behavior and determine which payment methods generate the most revenue.

**Business Action:** Optimize payment options and promotional offers based on regional and customer payment preferences.
